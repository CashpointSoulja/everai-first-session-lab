# Jobs to be done

These jobs are hypotheses for an independent concept. They are not interview findings.

| Situation | Job | Desired outcome |
|---|---|---|
| I am meeting a companion for the first time. | Help me start without a long setup. | A comfortable first exchange with little effort. |
| I have shared a small preference. | Carry it forward accurately. | I do not need to repeat myself. |
| A remembered detail is wrong. | Let me correct it where I notice it. | The next reply reflects my correction. |
| A detail no longer feels appropriate. | Let me stop its future use. | I can continue without that detail returning. |
| I decide whether to come back. | Show continuity without being presumptuous. | I feel understood and in control. |

## Functional, emotional and social dimensions

- Functional: correct, edit and forget a detail with predictable effects.
- Emotional: feel comfortable rather than monitored.
- Social: retain agency; the companion respects a choice to disclose less.

## Prototype mapping

The three light questions reduce setup. Message-eight review makes the choices visible. The memory panel explains their effect. A later scripted reply uses only retained details.

## Research questions before a real test

1. Which details do users want carried forward?
2. When does recall feel helpful versus premature?
3. Can users find Forget without guidance?
4. Does an empty memory feel acceptable, or does the interface pressure disclosure?
5. What do users think Forget removes: future use, a transcript, or everything?
