Our inference

Make remembering
visible from the start.

If the companion correctly carries a shared detail into its next reply, the opening may feel more personal and less repetitive.

That is the hypothesis for this concept. The review does not establish memory as the primary cause of first-session drop-off.

A little memory.
A better beginning?

Nova asks three light questions. At message eight, the treatment makes remembering visible—and lets the user decide what stays.

Control: the same three questions, followed by a generic next step.

Treatment: a three-detail review at message eight, with Keep, Edit and Forget.

Proposed primary signal: returning on day seven. Secondary signal: completing the first session. Incorrect recall and unwanted familiarity must not increase.

        Test with a small real cohort.
        Keep the rollout limited.

        This synthetic pattern would justify investigating the idea, not declaring a win.
